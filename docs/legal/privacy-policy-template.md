*Last updated: [date]*

[Service name] ("we") runs Little Wins, a points, stars and rewards chart for families, at [address]. This

**Who we are.** [Your name or organisation, postal address, and a contact email for privacy questions.]
[If you have appointed a data protection officer or an EU/UK representative, give their contact details.]

## Whose data this is

- **Parents** are our users. You sign in, set up your family, and decide what is recorded.
- **Children** never have an account and never type anything into Little Wins. What is recorded about a child
  is entered by their parents, and we process it on the parents' behalf and on their instructions.

## What we keep

| What | Why | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Your sign-in: the provider (Google or GitHub), the provider's ID for your account, your email address, login and display name | To sign you in and show who recorded what to the other parents in your family | The sign-in provider, when you sign in |
| Your family: its name, time zone, week start and language | To run your family's chart | You |
| Each child's first name or nickname | To tell your children's charts apart | You |
| Your behaviors, penalties, weekly challenges and rewards | To run your family's chart | You |
| What you record: points, warnings, penalties, challenge marks, with the time and which parent recorded each | This is the chart | You and the other parents in your family |
| Devices: whether each logged-in device is a parent's or a child's, a short description of it (such as "iPhone · Safari"), and when it was last used | So you can see and log out your family's devices | Your browser |

We do **not** ask for or keep children's birthdates, photos, schools, locations or contact details, and the app
has no place to enter them.

**Cookies.** Little Wins uses only cookies it needs to work: one that keeps a device signed in, and one that
lasts 10 minutes during sign-in. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so there is no
consent banner. [If you offer demo families: a demo uses its own cookie, which ends when the browser closes.]

**No third parties.** Little Wins loads no analytics, advertising, trackers, third-party scripts or
third-party fonts. Nothing about you or your children is sold or shared for marketing.

- **Delete** your family and everything in it (Settings → Delete this family, for the owner).
- **Log out** any device (Settings → Devices).

You can also write to [contact email] to ask for access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data, or to
