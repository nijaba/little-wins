Celebrate the small wins, one star at a time.

Little Wins is a points, stars and rewards tracker for families, made for phones. Parents record good moments and a few weekly challenges. Children watch their points and stars grow toward rewards the family agreed on.

The tour in 30 seconds

A parent opens a demo family and walks through the app's built-in tour, with a voice-over. Everyone in it is made up.

For parents: a few taps a day

Tap + when something good happens. Mark each weekly challenge perfect, OK or bad by tapping the day. A challenge wins a star in a week with at least two perfect days and no bad day. Fix a mark later and the stars follow.

  • Several children, each with their own points, stars and history.
  • Co-parents join with an invite link; each signs in with Google or GitHub.
  • The balance never goes below zero, and every entry can be undone.

For children: only the good news

The child's view shows points, stars, the next reward and a celebration when one unlocks. It never shows penalties, warnings or parents' notes, and no other child's data. A child's phone joins with a one-time code or link: no account, no password.

Private by default

No trackers, no third parties

No analytics, no third-party scripts, no external fonts. The only outsiders are the sign-in providers, which learn only that someone signed in to this app.

Minimal data about children

A child is a first name or a nickname. No birthdate, photo, school or contact details, and children cannot type anything into the app.

Yours to take or delete

The owner can export the family's data as JSON and delete the family, which removes every row at once.

Careful with the rest

Session tokens are stored only as hashes, logs never hold names or notes, and the app refuses to start with weak secrets.

Self-hosted or hosted

One container, one process, one SQLite file on a mounted volume, and no runtime dependencies. The same code serves both modes; the difference is configuration. For families who can't run a server, the author runs a free hosted service at little-wins.nijalabs.com, kept as private as possible, with a limited number of places.
